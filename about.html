About Vireo Interactive

Our Story

Vireo Interactive was founded in 2004 by a small group of developers who believed role-playing games could be remembered for more than combat.

Working from a modest office in Hoshimine, the team set out to create adventures built around memorable characters, comforting locations, and mysteries that rewarded curious players.

Our Philosophy

We believe players should care about a world before they are asked to save it.

  • Characters come first.
  • Curiosity should be rewarded.
  • Every location should tell a story.
  • Not every mystery requires an immediate answer.

Our Team

Vireo Interactive currently employs eleven developers, artists, writers, musicians, and support staff.

To protect the privacy of our team, individual staff information is not published publicly. Several members previously worked within larger development studios and prefer to let their work speak for itself.

Company statement:

“Our work should speak for itself. We ask that players remember our worlds, not the people who built them.”
